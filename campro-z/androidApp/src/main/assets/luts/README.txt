ProCam Z: Vintage 80s and Green Pastures

Two original 33 point CUBE LUTs for normalized nonlinear Rec.709 SDR RGB.
35,937 RGB entries per LUT, red index changes fastest.

VINTAGE 80s
A stylized faded print look: lifted blacks, softer whites, muted colors,
warm highlights and subtly cool shadows. Start at 60 to 80% intensity.
A LUT does not add grain, VHS lines, blur, glow or tape distortion.
Add those separately if desired. This is an original creative look, not a
calibrated emulation of a specific film stock or camera.

GREEN PASTURES
Richer foliage and a modest yellow-green shift toward fresh green, with
restrained effects on blue skies and neutral colors. Start at 60 to 80%.
Use 100% for a stronger green landscape. This is selective foliage grading,
not a green tint over the whole frame. Color selection cannot distinguish
objects with the same color; yellow-green clothing can also be affected.
It cannot turn genuinely brown or dry fields into realistic green grass.

USE
Extract the ZIP and import the desired .cube into your app LUT menu.
Your app must support 33 point 3D CUBE import and proper interpolation.
Apply only to Rec.709 SDR. Convert Log, HDR PQ, HLG or linear footage correctly
before applying. Match the RGB transfer function between preview and export.
Normalize video range correctly before evaluation. Do not expand range twice.
Adjust exposure and white balance before the creative LUT.

IMPORTANT FOR PROCAM Z
These files change color only. They cannot introduce geometric zoom, mirror
or stretching. If LUT selection changes framing or orientation, repair the
app rendering transform before judging these looks. Preview only versus
baking into recordings depends on your app implementation.

CHECKS
Both written files were parsed and checked for correct entry count, finite
values and 0 to 1 range. Green Pastures was checked for neutral preservation,
foliage hue direction and stable blue sample hue. ZIP integrity was checked.
These are starter looks, not tested on your phone or camera footage.
Test daylight faces, skies and grass before a release. VALIDATION.json
contains SHA256 checksums and entry counts.
